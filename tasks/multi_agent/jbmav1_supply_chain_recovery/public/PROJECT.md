# NX-440 supply-chain recovery

Three customer orders depend on the same NX-440 controller while inventory,
supplier quality, and freight records disagree about what can arrive safely and
on time. Build a decision-ready recovery package rather than merely explaining
the shortage.

Customer impact, supplier/quality disposition, and logistics modeling should
proceed in parallel. Reconcile all three streams into an allocation, customer
communication plan, and one owner-ready decision. A quality hold is not an
inventory pool. Do not promise freight spend, release held units, or send final
customer notices without documented authority.

The operations owner will not be available after the opening brief. If several
safe recovery paths remain, record a narrow question, retain a reversible
default, and complete every artifact.
