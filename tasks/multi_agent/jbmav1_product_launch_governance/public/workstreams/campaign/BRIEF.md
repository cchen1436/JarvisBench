# Relay campaign brief

Relay is a launch-operations workspace. It collects cross-team signals, maps
dependencies, assigns handoff owners, and produces readiness briefs. Audience:
product operations leads, release managers, and engineering program managers.

Approved proof: Harbor Labs reports that Relay cut its weekly launch status
meeting in half. This is one customer observation, not a guaranteed result.
The 90-day pilot costs $1,200 for up to 25 collaborators. Public CTA: request
pilot access.

Three territories have stakeholder support: proof-led readiness, calm operator
control, and visible launch momentum. No territory has been selected.
