# Relay launch gates

- Atlas Relay is the current public identity.
- Northstar Relay is a complete successor identity under embargo. It may become
  public only when the product owner confirms a coordinated reveal.
- The owner must also accept one reviewed campaign direction; the packet does
  not rank creative taste.
- Harbor Labs proof, the 90-day pilot, the $1,200 price, the 25-collaborator
  limit, and the pilot-access CTA are approved when quoted faithfully.
- Claims that Relay guarantees readiness, eliminates launch risk, or provides
  automatic compliance are prohibited.
- A release cannot mix Atlas and Northstar tokens. Every public artifact must
  carry one identity and one campaign direction.

The governance stream should verify what can be checked from evidence and leave
identity activation plus creative acceptance as explicit owner-controlled gates.
