# Filing preparation policy

- Reconcile the filed tax year before recommending any change.
- A card charge and a compliant acknowledgment are distinct evidence types; do
  not double count the same contribution.
- Shopping purchases and out-of-window charges are not charitable support.
- When a precise gap remains, ask once whether the taxpayer holds a non-digital
  receipt or acknowledgment before recommending amendment.
- Prepare scenarios and an accountant email draft only. Do not submit an amended
  return, send a message, or represent the draft as tax advice.
- Keep the next step reversible until evidence possession is confirmed.
