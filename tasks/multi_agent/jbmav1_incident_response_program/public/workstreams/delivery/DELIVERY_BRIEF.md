# Quarterly delivery constraints

Turn each candidate control into an executable, auditable program. The quarter
has three shared change windows: weeks 2, 6, and 10. A funded initiative may use
only one primary window and one follow-up verification window.

Available teams:

- Release Engineering: 8 delivery points, owns staged rollout controls.
- SRE: 10 delivery points, owns observability and participates in recovery.
- Database: 6 delivery points, owns restore validation.
- Platform: 5 delivery points, supports policy and telemetry integration.

Every option must name dependencies, a dry-run or exercise, pass/fail evidence,
and an organizational risk. All three candidate initiatives are technically
possible within the quarter, but only one receives the first funded slot. Do
not infer the service owner's external commitments or priority.
