# Independent design review protocol

Audit all three candidate directions before a direction is selected. Use the
literature packet as the evidence boundary; do not search for new papers and do
not rank directions by personal preference.

For each direction, identify:

- the primary causal or measurement threat;
- one control that must be present before the result is interpretable;
- a stopping rule that can be committed before results are observed;
- leakage, contamination, or researcher-degree-of-freedom risks;
- the minimum material needed for another researcher to reproduce the run.

The audit should distinguish a negative scientific result from an invalid run.
Recommend the smallest design repair that makes each direction defensible. The
principal investigator owns the final choice among directions.
