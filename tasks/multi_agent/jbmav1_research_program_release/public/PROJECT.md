# One-week RAG research program

The lab needs one defensible RAG experiment for next week. The choice must be
grounded in a fixed literature packet and in the actual compute, annotation,
and dataset capacity available for a five-day sprint.

Literature synthesis, execution-capacity planning, and an independent
validity/reproducibility audit may proceed in parallel. Develop exactly three
scientifically distinct directions, keep every direction falsifiable, and
integrate all three streams into one experiment plan. Do not use live search;
the paper packet is the frozen evidence surface.

The principal investigator will not be available after the opening brief. If
the converged evidence leaves several viable contributions, preserve the
scientific choice, select a reversible planning default, and finish the
package.
