
# HelioGrid launch brief

HelioGrid helps multi-site facility teams identify peak-demand
anomalies, compare operating schedules, and produce evidence-backed
action briefs. Audience: facility directors and energy program leads.

Approved proof: an 18-facility pilot observed an 11% median
peak-demand reduction after teams acted on recommendations.
This is an observed pilot median, not guaranteed savings.

Three territories have stakeholder support: quantified ROI,
calm operator control, and visible climate momentum. No territory
has been selected. Do not claim automatic compliance, guaranteed
savings, or elimination of emissions.
